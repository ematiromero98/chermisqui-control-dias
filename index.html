How it works
From phone to calendar in four steps
No app to install, no spreadsheets. A shared calendar that stays up to date on its own.
1

Request from the phone

The employee scans a QR code (or opens a link), picks their name, the type of leave and the dates.

2

It arrives as pending

The request shows up in the dashboard as “pending approval”, with the person's balance in view.

3

Manager approves

You approve or reject in one click. The employee's remaining days update automatically.

4

Everyone sees it

It lands on the year calendar, the monthly view and the analytics — with automatic overlap alerts.

5

Plan ahead

Peak months, per-person balances and coverage gaps, plus one-click Excel/PDF exports.

Self-service, zero friction

Employees don't need an account or an app. They open the link (or scan the QR we print for the office) and submit in under a minute.

  • ✅ Picks their name → sees days assigned, taken and remaining right away.
  • ✅ Sees the dates they already have booked, so no duplicates.
  • ✅ The request reaches you as pending — nothing is final until you approve.
  • ✅ Try it: submit a request here and watch it appear in the dashboard above.

Tip: in the real office we print a one-page sheet with a QR code that opens this form.

Why it helps
What we get out of it
🛡️

No coverage gaps

Automatic alerts when two people from the same team are out at the same time — protect busy periods like closings and filing deadlines.

⚡

Less back-and-forth

Requests, approvals and balances live in one place. No emails or spreadsheets to reconcile.

👀

Instant visibility

Who's out today, this month, and how the year looks — for everyone, always current.

📊

Planning analytics

Peak months, average days per person and % of the team that booked time off — anticipate staffing.

🧮

Accurate balances

Days assigned vs. taken and remaining, computed automatically — no manual tallies.

💵

No license cost

Built in-house and runs on our own database. Exports to Excel and PDF for records.
